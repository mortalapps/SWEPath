Privacy Policy: SWEPath

Last updated: September 9, 2026

SWEPath ("the app") is provided by MortalApps ("we", "us", "our"). The app is designed with a privacy-conscious approach and aims to minimize unnecessary collection of user data wherever possible.

SWEPath is a technical interview preparation app that uses spaced repetition to help you study topics across role based roadmaps, with quizzes, streaks, and a review schedule that adapts to how well you know each topic.

1. Information Handling Overview

1.1 Personal Data

We do not require user accounts and do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Typed answers or free-form text you enter, except where you explicitly submit it to us (see 1.5, Content and Question Reports)

1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

Most learning progress and activity data is stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Review history and spaced-repetition scheduling data (FSRS)
  • Your selected roadmap and topic-attempt history
  • The section of a topic you last read, so a topic reopens where you left off
  • Daily and current/longest streaks, and streak shield status
  • Daily Challenge completion status
  • App settings (theme, font size, accessibility options, notification preferences)
  • Premium entitlement status

This locally stored data generally remains on your device unless you:

  • Delete the app
  • Clear app storage/data
  • Reset your device
  • Use Reset All Progress within the app (see 1.7)

1.3 Usage Analytics

With your permission where required by applicable law, SWEPath may collect analytics and technical diagnostics information to understand app usage, improve learning features, measure engagement, support premium functionality, optimize monetization, and help sustain the app experience.

Analytics data falls into the following general categories:

  • Learning engagement events, such as onboarding, topic views, quizzes, roadmap progress, and streaks
  • Monetization events, such as paywall views and purchase activity, including transaction value and currency (see 4.1 for how this may be used with Google Ads)
  • Ad funnel events, such as when a rewarded ad is shown, completed, or dismissed
  • App-sharing actions
  • App stability and crash diagnostics

Analytics data is generally behavioral and technical in nature and is not intended to directly identify individual users. Purchase analytics events may include the monetary value and currency of a transaction; this is used for business reporting purposes (see 1.4) and is not linked to payment card or banking details, which we never receive (see 4.3).

We do not intentionally use analytics to:

  • Track the specific topics or quiz answers you interact with at an individual level for advertising purposes
  • Record detailed question-by-question behavioral replay
  • Collect sensitive personal information through analytics events
  • Sell personal information to third parties

Analytics collection can be turned off from within the app (Settings, "Share Anonymous Analytics"), subject to technical and platform limitations. Crash and stability diagnostics (see 1.4) are handled separately from this setting, since they support basic app reliability rather than behavioral analytics.

1.4 Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics & Google Services

SWEPath uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and related Google services to collect usage analytics, app diagnostics, engagement metrics, and monetization insights.

Firebase and related Google services may automatically collect technical information including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Approximate country or region
  • Anonymous app instance identifiers
  • Crash and performance diagnostics
  • Advertising and conversion-related identifiers where supported

Crashlytics diagnostic data is collected to keep the app stable and is not gated behind the analytics setting described in 1.3, since it supports basic reliability rather than behavioral analytics.

We do not intentionally use analytics data to directly identify individual users.

1.5 Content and Question Reports

If you choose to report a topic, quiz question, or other content issue, the app transmits:

  • Topic or question identifier
  • Selected reason for the report
  • Optional free-text details you choose to type

Reports are used solely to review and improve content quality and accuracy.

Please do not include personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or other identifying details) in the free-text details field. This field is optional and intended only for describing the content issue itself.

1.6 AI-Assisted Learning Content

Topic overviews, key concepts, architecture explanations, common mistakes, production considerations, quiz questions, and related learning materials within the app are generated or assisted using artificial intelligence (AI) systems.

While we strive to review and improve content quality, this material may still contain inaccuracies, omissions, ambiguities, or outdated information. The app is intended for interview-preparation and self-study purposes only and should not be relied upon as professional, hiring, or career advice.

1.7 Reset, Export, and Import

Export Progress (Premium) lets you save your locally stored review history, quiz and topic-attempt history, active roadmap, streak, and app settings as a file you control, for backup or transfer to another device. It does not include your Premium entitlement status, purchase information, or analytics consent preference. Import Progress (Premium) restores from a file you previously exported. These files are created and stored by you, using your device's own file storage or sharing options; we do not receive a copy.

Reset All Progress permanently deletes your review history, streak data, and quiz history from your device. It does not affect your Premium entitlement status, which is managed independently through Google Play (see 4.3).

Firebase and Google services are operated by Google.

4.2 Google AdMob

SWEPath uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads (to unlock topic content and practice quizzes) and native ads (in topic browsing lists). Whether an ad is personalized depends on your region and, where applicable, your choice:

  • In the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before showing personalized ads. Ads are shown without personalization if you do not consent, or before you respond.
  • In US states with applicable privacy laws, UMP presents an opt-out choice; ads are personalized unless you opt out.
  • In India, every ad is always requested as non-personalized, regardless of any choice, since we have no way to verify a user's age and India's DPDP Act prohibits targeted advertising to minors (see 6.1).
  • Outside these regions, ads may be personalized by default.

You can review or change your ad consent choice anytime from Settings, "Manage Ad Consent" (shown only where a choice applies to you).

4.3 Google Play Billing

Purchases are processed securely through Google Play. We do not receive or store payment card information, banking details, or billing credentials. Your Premium purchase is verified directly with Google Play; SWEPath does not use a third-party subscription-management service.

4.4 Content Report Submission

Optional topic and quiz issue reports are transmitted securely via HTTPS for review and content-quality improvement.

5. Data Retention

Most learning progress data remains locally on your device.

Analytics, diagnostics, and advertising-related information may be retained by Google and Firebase according to their applicable retention policies. Premium entitlement records are retained by Google Play for as long as needed to manage your access.

We do not maintain traditional personal user accounts or cloud-based personal learning profiles.

5.1 Automatic Backup via Google Block Store

SWEPath may use Google Block Store, a Google Play Services feature, to back up your Premium entitlement and any active ad-earned topic unlocks tied to your Google account, so they can be restored if you reinstall the app or set up a new device signed in with the same Google account. This requires Google Play Services and a signed-in Google account; if either is unavailable, this backup is silently skipped and the app continues to work normally from local data. Manually clearing app data, or reinstalling on a different Google Account, does not restore this data automatically; use Export/Import (see 1.7) to migrate progress in those cases.

6. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights relating to:

  • Access to your data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (deletion) of your data
  • Withdrawal of analytics consent
  • Grievance redressal, and the right to nominate someone to act on your behalf

Since SWEPath does not maintain traditional user accounts or directly identifiable personal learning profiles, many requests may already be satisfied by:

  • Disabling analytics in Settings
  • Resetting advertising identifiers in your device settings
  • Using Export Progress to obtain a copy of your locally stored data (see 1.7)
  • Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app

To raise a grievance or ask a question about how your data is handled, contact us using the details in Section 11. We aim to acknowledge grievances promptly and will respond within the time frame required by applicable law.

6.1 Children's Data

SWEPath is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. We do not ask for or collect date of birth or other age information, and we have no way to verify a user's age. As a protective measure specific to India's DPDP Act, every ad shown to users in India is always non-personalized (see 4.2), regardless of age. Outside India, ad personalization instead follows the region-specific consent framework described in 4.2, since we have no reliable way to identify minors outside a jurisdiction that legally requires this protection.
